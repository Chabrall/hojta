Hojta

Hold a key, say it, and it plays in the room you picked — on the Sonos speakers you already own. No account, nothing leaves your home.

Support

Something not working? Email SUPPORT_EMAIL_TBD and include your iPhone model, iOS version and which Sonos speakers you have.

Hojta needs at least one Sonos speaker on the same Wi-Fi as your iPhone. If the app says NOT FOUND, check that the phone is on the same network as the speakers (not a guest network) and tap Rescan.
