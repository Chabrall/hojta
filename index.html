Questions

Do I need a Sonos account?

No. Hojta finds the speakers on your Wi-Fi directly — there is nothing to sign in to.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything happens on your local network, even if the internet is down.

What happens to the music that was playing?

It pauses, your message plays once, and it resumes right where it left off.

How many rooms are free?

One, forever. The first room you send to becomes your free room. Whole House unlocks every room, once, shared with your family.
