Hojta — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Hojta is an iPhone app that plays short voice messages on Sonos speakers over your home Wi-Fi.

What Hojta collects

Nothing. Hojta has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no server of its own.

Where your voice goes

When you hold the talk key, your iPhone records a clip of up to 30 seconds. The clip is served from your iPhone directly to the Sonos speaker you picked, over your local Wi-Fi network, and is deleted from the phone as soon as the speaker has played it. It never leaves your network and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Permissions Hojta asks for

  • Microphone — to record the message while you hold the key.
  • Local network — to find Sonos speakers on your Wi-Fi and send messages to them.

What Hojta stores on your iPhone

One setting: the message volume you chose. That is all.

Third parties

Hojta talks only to Sonos speakers on your own network. It does not use any third-party SDKs. Hojta is not affiliated with Sonos, Inc. Sonos is a trademark of Sonos, Inc.
